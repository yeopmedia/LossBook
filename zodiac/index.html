Where accounts differ

Where the records disagree, we follow the document made closest to the event, and say so here.

  • Where the cab stopped. The San Francisco police report puts Yellow Cab #912 at the corner of Washington and Cherry Streets (SFPD pp.1–2). The letter of October 13, 1969 named a different corner (L3). The narration follows the police report.
  • Whether officers stopped him. His November 1969 letter, as the Chronicle reported it, claimed that officers had stopped and questioned him; the city's chief of inspectors called that "poppycock" in the same article (C3 p.22). Patrolman Fouke's memo of November 12, 1969 says the man he saw was not stopped, because the description broadcast by radio was of a Black man (MEMO). The narration gives the letter as his claim and the memo as the record.
  • When the first cipher was cracked. Some later accounts give an earlier date in August 1969. The FBI file has the Vallejo Times-Herald report of August 9, 1969, enclosed with an FBI airtel (V1 pp.20–21), so the narration gives that date for the report.
  • When "The Confession" was sent. A 1974 FBI memo says it was sent in "the first part of 1967" (V4 pp.161–163). The envelope examined by the FBI Laboratory is postmarked "RIVERSIDE, CALIF NOV 29 PM 1966" (V4 pp.165–166). The narration follows the postmark.
  • The 1971 letter about Riverside. A 1974 FBI memo mentions a letter to the Chronicle in early 1971 (V4 pp.161–163). The state Department of Justice report says the letter was mailed in Pleasanton to the Los Angeles Times on March 13, 1971 (DOJ2 scan p.7). The narration follows the state report.
  • How many suspects. Some later reports give 2,500. The 1976 Chronicle article reports "the names of more than 2000 potential suspects" in the San Francisco files (C5), which is the number the narration uses.
  • The FBI and the 340 solution. Some reports say the FBI "verified" the solution. Its own statement of December 11, 2020 says: "The FBI is aware that a cipher attributed to the Zodiac Killer was recently solved by private citizens," and calls the investigation ongoing (FBISF). The narration follows the statement.
