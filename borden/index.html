Where accounts differ

Where the records disagree, we follow the document made closest to the event, and say so here.

  • How long the jury was out. Some later accounts give 40 minutes or an hour and a half. The stenographic report has the jury retiring at twenty-four minutes past three and coming back in at twenty-eight minutes before five, about 68 minutes (T2 pp.1927–1930). The New Bedford Evening Standard reported about an hour (NBES issue of June 21, 1893, p.2 (PDF pp.351–356, 365–366)), Porter an hour and ten minutes (POR chs. 22–32), and Wigmore less than an hour and a half (WIG pp.819–845). The narration says a little over an hour.
  • When Abby Borden died. The doctors at the trial gave different intervals between the two deaths, from at least an hour to several hours (T1 pp.832–833; T1 pp.885–908, 920–924, 965–979; T2 pp.989–998, 1019–1020; T2 pp.1031–1059; T2 pp.1086–1109), and one allowed under cross-examination that she might have died at nine or at ten (T2 pp.1020–1030). All of them put her death first. The narration says an hour or more before Andrew.
  • Abby Borden's age. The autopsy record read at the preliminary hearing gives 67, and Dr. Dolan's testimony at that hearing, as reported, gives 53 (NBES issues of August 25, 1892, p.1; August 26, 1892, pp.1, 2, 8 (PDF pp.135–138, 143–147, 152–157)); the first newspaper reports give 62 (NBES issues of August 4, 1892, pp.1, 6; August 5, 1892, p.2 (PDF pp.2–7)); at the trial Dr. Dolan said about 64 (T1 pp.885–908, 920–924, 965–979). She was born in 1828 (FRHSD), so the narration gives 64.
  • When the Globe reporter died. Porter says Trickey was killed by a railroad train in Canada in the latter part of November 1892 (POR ch. 21); a grand juror quoted in the Standard of December 5, 1892 says only that he was dead (NBES issues of December 2, 1892, p.1; December 5, 1892, p.1 (PDF pp.277–282)); Pearson says he was killed by a train in Canada soon after (PEAR introduction, pp.1–93, and notes). No newspaper of the time that we read gives the date, so the narration gives it as a later account.
